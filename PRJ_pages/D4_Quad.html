ELECTRIC QUAD CLUSTER & INFOTAINMENT

Prototype off-road electric quad Cluster & Infotainment

Full featured electronics development for an off-road electric quad prototype, including a cluster and infotainment display, implementing my own design elements and functionalities.



Phase 1: Idea & Research
Project Scope
This project regards the realization from the concept to the final product of the complete dashboard electronics, including a cluster and infotainment box, for a prototypal electric off-road quad with full carbon fiber body.
This prototype is used to improve the design for a future pre-serie, having as final target the mass production of the vehicle.


Preliminary activity
Preliminary tests and checks were performed to verify the integration between the choosen electronic platform and car electrical interface exposed by the electric motor control unit (MCU).
Phase 2 - Design & UX
UI development
> UI design development is inspired to Electric Hummer stock cluster for both cluster and infotainment display.
> This involved the design of custom made widgets for the various indicators.

PC side development of a preliminary SW for the basic functions, for:

CLuster
> Data collection from vehicle electric motor ECU's CAN bus interface, and external sensors
> Real time data display on cluster, with indication of:
  - Vehicle speed, odometer
  - Gearshift, low/high beam lights, turn indicators, hanbrake
  - Battery pack voltage and current levels, battery charge level estimation, electric motor temperature
> implementation of battery manager strategies (BMS) and functionalities for battery charge level prediction and indication, and battery recharge features.

Infotainment
> Third-party Android Auto software integration, providing connectivity with an Android smartphone applications.
> GPS navigation capability is provided by Android Auto implementation
> HVAC graphic elements integration in the UI and design study for a HVAC commands future implementation, including:
  - Temperature settings
  - Ventilation Fan Speed
  - Air flow selector, air recirculation

A Demo mode was implemented as well, for an animated presentation of the UI to the customer.
Phase 3 - Prototyping
>Last step is the porting of the SW on the target platform, (based on commercial HW), doing the final adjustments to adapt the UI to the real touch screen display and the softwre to the target board embedded peripherals.

> A demo mode (used as well for testing during the sw development) was implemented using a Vector CANalyzer to execute a CAPL program that simulated the CAN bus communication of the MCU to stimulate the cluster.

> It was also the opportunity for a Boot time optimization, obtained overclocking the device, and disabling unused O.S. services.
